Our Story

Pilsen's Neighborhood Barbershop

We've been cutting hair right here on Cermak Road for years, serving the families, workers, and community of Pilsen and the surrounding South Side neighborhoods. This is more than a haircut — it's the chair you come back to.

Our barbers bring old-school technique with modern precision. No rush, no shortcuts. Every client walks out looking clean and feeling good. That's the standard we hold ourselves to every single day.
